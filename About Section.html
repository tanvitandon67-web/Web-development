About Daddy Pig

A little bit about him

Daddy Pig is a beloved, jovial character from the animated children's series Peppa Pig,> voiced by Richard Ridings. He is easily recognized by his teal-green shirt, black glasses, brown moustache and beard, and cheerful, self-proclaimed status as an "expert" at almost everything—from map reading and DIY to claiming his large tummy is "pure muscle". Married to Mummy Pig, he is the proud father of Peppa, George, and Evie. Working as a structural engineer or architect who calculates load-bearing tangents, Daddy Pig balances an office career with being a playful, resilient, and patient father figure who famously holds the title of world-record muddy puddle jumping champion.


Despite his boundless confidence, Daddy Pig frequently finds himself in comical predicaments due to his stubbornness,such as getting hopelessly lost while refusing to use a sat-nav or accidentally shrinking his favourite football shirt in the wash. Yet, he never lets these mishaps ruin his good spirits, always laughing off his mistakes with his signature cheerful snort Whether he is enthusiastically playing the accordion, cheering on his kids, or enjoying a giant slice of chocolate cake, Daddy Pig's warmth and good-natured humour make him an endearing and relatable dad to audiences of all ages.
